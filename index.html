Indian History is one of the highest-scoring sections of the General Awareness paper in SSC and Railway exams, and the same set of facts — the first Harappan site, the founder of the Mauryan Empire, the battles that changed Indian rule — return year after year in CGL, CHSL, MTS and NTPC papers. This guide lines up those repeated questions era by era so you can revise them in one sitting, then test yourself with the MCQ quiz below before exam day.

Ancient India

Indus Valley sites, Vedic literature, the Mauryas and Guptas, and early religious movements.

01
Which Indus Valley site was excavated first, and by whom?
Harappa, dug up by Dayaram Sahni in 1921 — this discovery gave the civilisation its name.
02
Which Harappan city served as its major dockyard and port?
Lothal, in present-day Gujarat, had a purpose-built dock for maritime trade.
03
Where was the famous "Great Bath" of the Indus Valley Civilization found?
Mohenjo-daro, unearthed by R.D. Banerji in 1922.
04
Which is the oldest of the four Vedas?
The Rigveda, which contains the Gayatri Mantra.
05
Who founded the Mauryan Empire, and who mentored him?
Chandragupta Maurya founded the empire, guided by his advisor Chanakya (Kautilya).
06
Who wrote the ancient political treatise Arthashastra?
Kautilya — also known as Chanakya or Vishnugupta.
07
Which Greek ambassador wrote Indica after visiting Chandragupta Maurya's court?
Megasthenes, sent by Seleucus I Nicator.
08
Which Mauryan emperor turned to Buddhism after the Kalinga War?
Emperor Ashoka, following the war in 261 BCE.
09
Who was the last ruler of the Mauryan Empire?
Brihadratha, overthrown by his own general Pushyamitra Shunga.
10
Which Gupta emperor is called the "Napoleon of India"?
Samudragupta, a title given to him by historian V.A. Smith for his military conquests.
11
Who founded the ancient Nalanda Mahavihara university?
Kumaragupta I of the Gupta dynasty.
12
Which Chinese pilgrim visited India during Harshavardhana's reign?
Xuanzang (Hiuen Tsang), who documented Indian society in detail.
13
Where did Gautama Buddha deliver his first sermon?
At Sarnath — an event known as the Dharmachakrapravartana.
14
Who was the 24th and last Jain Tirthankara?
Vardhamana Mahavira.
15
Who built the rock-cut Kailasa Temple at Ellora?
Rashtrakuta king Krishna I, carving it from a single rock.

Medieval India

The Delhi Sultanate, Vijayanagara, and the rise and consolidation of the Mughal Empire.

16
Who led the first Arab conquest of Sindh, in 712 CE?
Muhammad bin Qasim.
17
Who won the Second Battle of Tarain (1192 CE)?
Muhammad Ghori defeated Prithviraj Chauhan.
18
Who founded the Delhi Sultanate in 1206 CE?
Qutb-ud-din Aibak, starting the Slave (Mamluk) dynasty.
19
Who was the only woman to rule the Delhi Sultanate?
Razia Sultana.
20
Which Delhi Sultan introduced the Dagh and Chehra market-control systems?
Alauddin Khalji.
21
Which Sultan shifted the Delhi capital to Daulatabad?
Muhammad bin Tughlaq.
22
Who founded the Vijayanagara Empire in 1336 CE?
Harihara I and Bukka Raya I.
23
Who was the most celebrated Vijayanagara ruler?
Krishnadevaraya of the Tuluva dynasty.
24
Which battle established the Mughal Empire in India?
The First Battle of Panipat (1526), where Babur defeated Ibrahim Lodi.
25
Who did Babur defeat at the Battle of Khanwa (1527)?
Rana Sanga of Mewar.
26
Who built the Grand Trunk Road and introduced the silver Rupiya?
Sher Shah Suri.
27
Who won the Second Battle of Panipat (1556)?
Akbar's forces, led by Bairam Khan, defeated Hemu.
28
Who designed Akbar's revenue assessment system, the Ain-i-Dahsala?
Raja Todar Mal.
29
Under which Mughal emperor did miniature painting reach its peak?
Jahangir.
30
Which Mughal emperor built the Red Fort, Jama Masjid and Taj Mahal?
Shah Jahan.
31
Which Mughal emperor carried the title Alamgir?
Aurangzeb.
32
In which year was Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj crowned at Raigad Fort?
1674 CE.
33
Who defeated the Marathas at the Third Battle of Panipat (1761)?
Ahmad Shah Durrani.

Modern India

European arrival, Company rule, the 1857 revolt, and the freedom struggle.

34
Who was the first European to reach India by sea, in 1498?
Vasco da Gama, landing at Calicut.
35
Which battle gave the British East India Company political control of Bengal?
The Battle of Plassey (1757), where Robert Clive defeated Siraj-ud-Daulah.
36
Which battle led to the Treaty of Allahabad and Diwani rights in 1765?
The Battle of Buxar (1764).
37
Who introduced the Permanent Settlement of Bengal in 1793?
Governor-General Lord Cornwallis.
38
Which annexation policy did Lord Dalhousie introduce?
The Doctrine of Lapse.
39
Who is credited with sparking the 1857 revolt at Barrackpore?
Sepoy Mangal Pandey.
40
Who led the 1857 revolt in the Arrah–Jagdishpur region of Bihar?
Veer Kunwar Singh.
41
Who campaigned to abolish Sati, banned in 1829?
Raja Ram Mohan Roy, working with Governor-General Lord William Bentinck.
42
Who founded the Indian National Congress in 1885?
Allan Octavian Hume.
43
Which Viceroy ordered the 1905 Partition of Bengal?
Lord Curzon.
44
Which was Gandhi's first satyagraha on Indian soil?
The Champaran Satyagraha (1917), in Bihar.
45
Who was responsible for the Jallianwala Bagh Massacre of April 1919?
Brigadier-General Reginald Dyer.
46
When did Gandhi's Dandi March begin?
12 March 1930, starting from Sabarmati Ashram.
47
What was agreed in the 1932 Poona Pact?
An agreement between Gandhi and Dr. B.R. Ambedkar on separate electorates for depressed classes.
48
During which movement did Gandhi give the "Do or Die" call?
The Quit India Movement (1942).
49
Who led the Indian National Army (Azad Hind Fauj)?
Subhas Chandra Bose, building on the force formed with Rash Behari Bose.
50
Who was independent India's first and last Governor-General?
C. Rajagopalachari.
